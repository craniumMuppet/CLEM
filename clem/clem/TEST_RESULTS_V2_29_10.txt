Scientific limitation retained deliberately
--------------------------------------------
The March 1979-2020 native-area temporal response is not scientifically adequate
under the predeclared diagnostic: the model trend is about 3.17 times the observed
trend and the correlation is about 0.19. This result is explicit, non-release-
blocking, and prevents temporal-skill or predictive-validation claims. Historical
sea-ice observations through March 2026 were inspected during development; no
retrospective independent temporal validation is claimed. The earliest reserved
prospective temporal evaluation begins in 2027.

The development benchmark group therefore does not pass every descriptive target.
AMOC, Greenland, future sea-ice area/extent, and ice-free timing remain reduced-
complexity sensitivity outputs rather than quantitative forecasts.
