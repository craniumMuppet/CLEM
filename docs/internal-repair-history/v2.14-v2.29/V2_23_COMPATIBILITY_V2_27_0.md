# v2.23 compatibility and intentional v2.27 behavior changes

## Preservation result

The dedicated maintained v2.23 regression module passes unchanged against the v2.27 core:

- branch assignment and secant prediction;
- pseudo-arclength continuation;
- root-search saturation diagnostics;
- exception propagation;
- complex-mode nonlinear stability testing;
- benchmark provenance structure;
- ocean heat-content diagnostics.

The complete default isolated v2.27 suite also passes. Optional Hypothesis property tests require the development dependency to be installed and are reported separately.

## Intentional structural differences

v2.27 is source- and workflow-compatible with the maintained v2.23 architecture, but it is a new structural model family. Numerical trajectories are not expected to match v2.23 because:

1. land and ocean absolute climatologies are now separated;
2. the Arctic ocean baseline is freezing-bounded north of 66 N;
3. the control sea-ice cycle is generated by a periodic energy balance rather than inherited mean ice mass;
4. Arctic amplification uses prognostic seasonal air/interface states and polar-darkness-weighted transport;
5. Greenland freshwater is split into explicit seasonal SMB and slow dynamic discharge;
6. the AMOC thermal-density coupling and convection-density scale are versioned for the corrected climatology;
7. benchmark evidence is explicitly marked `used_for_tuning=true`.

## Freshwater compatibility

Normal defaults remain:

- hydrological freshwater: 0.006 Sv/K;
- public Greenland coefficient: 0.005 Sv/K.

The legacy combined `warming_freshwater_sv_per_k` input remains accepted. When supplied, it disables both separated Greenland branches so old one-coefficient workflows do not silently acquire the v2.27 SMB response.

## Arctic compatibility controls

- `seasonal_arctic_enabled=false` disables the prognostic seasonal Arctic anomaly subsystem for diagnostic comparisons.
- Legacy empirical Arctic multiplier fields remain accepted from old configuration files but are hidden from normal interfaces.
- With the seasonal thermodynamic subsystem enabled, those legacy multipliers have zero numerical effect.

## Hosing behavior

A sustained 0.10 Sv hosing for 250 years now approaches the weak/collapsed branch but remains distinct from the nearly zero-AMOC 0.50 Sv experiment. A finite 40-year 0.10 Sv pulse remains reversible and recovers about 87% of its initial loss after 100 years without hosing.

This differs from the old v2.23 smoke threshold that required the weak sustained case to remain above 1 Sv. The revised check reflects the versioned density normalization and tests graded nonlinear response rather than preserving an obsolete endpoint.

## Long-horizon pathway behavior

The final v2.27 calibration keeps the default SSP2-4.5 and hybrid pathways on an active AMOC branch in the retained multi-century regression checks. The older v2.18.2 capped-CO2 stress test also no longer requires a crossing below the fixed 6 Sv reference: the 1200 ppm experiment reaches approximately 6.97 Sv and recovers gradually without the former 36.7 Sv restart pulse. This is an intentional numerical-trajectory difference from earlier structural families, so long-horizon posterior results must be rerun and labeled by model version.

## Interpretation

Use v2.27 for new experiments and rerun posterior/Monte Carlo ensembles. Do not combine v2.23 and v2.27 trajectories as members of one calibrated ensemble without explicitly treating model version as a structural uncertainty dimension.
