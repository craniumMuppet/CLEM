# R17 baseline evidence carried forward

R17 starts from the completed R16/R16.2 numerical evidence.

- Validated/default linear AMOC: 0.1/0.2/0.3 Sv hosing ended near 13.78/9.51/4.13 Sv.
- SSP2-4.5 ended near 8.25 Sv at 10 degrees and 8.83 Sv at 5 degrees.
- R16 freshwater/salt conservation and timestep convergence were clean.
- The South-Atlantic-upper hydraulic replacement was rejected and remains sensitivity-only.
- R16 sea-ice extent remained too diffuse, motivating the R17 support state.
- The R16 zero-hosing post-collapse branch did not recover through year 700; R17 maps de-hosing recovery rather than inserting a restart switch.
- R16.2 direct surface-watermass TEOS was substantially less sensitive than linear under forcing, motivating the matched-pathway TEOS branch.

These are comparison baselines, not new R17 validation results.
